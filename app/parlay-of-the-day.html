How the picks are made

Market first: Claude compares every FanDuel price with Pinnacle, the sharpest sportsbook, whose prices (with the house cut removed) are the best public estimate of each bet's real chance. A bet only makes the app when FanDuel pays at least about what it's worth there; game logs, injuries and news can nudge that by a couple of points, never more. A separate auditor re-checks every price, injury and stat before anything posts. Results grade themselves the next morning, and the Profit tab shows how the picks are really doing.

What do the bet types mean?
Big BetOne straight bet. Highest win chance with a real edge, so it handles a bigger stake best.
Power PairTwo strong legs that are too pricey alone but pay about even money or better together.
3-Leg ParlayOne per sport. The combo closest to a lock that still pays about 2-to-1.

Each bet shows the sportsbook with the best price; a parlay's legs are all at one book. Boosts (its own tab) are FanDuel promos with the best bet to use them on.

Best Bets board: single bets ranked by win chance and payout together. ★ means 55%+ to win and pays $18+ on $10.

Profit boosts

Each morning the research run reads today's FanDuel promos and picks the bet with the best value for each boost. A boost raises the winnings, not the stake, so it can turn a fair bet into a good one. Check the boost's terms in the app before you bet.
